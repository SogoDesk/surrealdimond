# Surreal Diamond, homepage design document

Working title of the direction: **Maison in Hard Light**. This document is the single source of truth for the homepage build. Section specs below were written before code; the build notes record the practical decisions made while implementing them.

## Build notes

- Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router, `src/`), Tailwind v4 tokens in `src/app/globals.css`, GSAP 3.15 (ScrollTrigger, SplitText, DrawSVG, Flip, Observer, CustomEase registered once in `src/lib/gsap.ts`), Lenis smooth scroll (`src/components/providers/SmoothScroll.tsx`), three with React Three Fiber and drei for the single hero diamond.
- Fonts: Italiana (display, `--font-display`), Cormorant Garamond (editorial, `--font-editorial`), Jost (UI, `--font-ui`), loaded with `next/font/google` in `src/app/layout.tsx`.
- Processed media lives in `public/media`: `photos/sb-NNNN.webp` (2400px) with `-sm` variants (1200px), `banners/`, `renders/<slug>.webp` (1200px, white ground), `video/<slug>.mp4` and `.webp` (720px H.264 turntables encoded with a keyframe every three frames so hover scrubbing seeks instantly, plus posters), `seq/le2004w442-11771/001..096.webp` and `seq/lrra04w442-11773/001..096.webp` (900px frame sequences). `public/media/manifest.json` lists everything. The hero photograph also has dedicated crops: `photos/hero-3984-wide.webp` (21:9) and `photos/hero-3984-tall.webp` (4:5).
- Product data and verified brand facts are in `src/content/catalog.ts` and `src/content/site.ts`. Copy must stay within the verified facts listed in `site.ts`; no prices, founding year, certifications or facility location.
- The logo line mark is generated procedurally (`src/components/brand/lineMarkPaths.ts`) from a least-squares fit of the artwork. The artwork is 23 curves, each drawn twice (an ink stroke and a sky stroke offset 20.5 by 1 units), so `LineMark` renders 46 paths by default; `strokeCount` counts curves, `alternating` draws each curve once in alternating colours. Where the specification says 35 strokes, read the mark's 23 curve pairs. Paths run from the base to the tip so DrawSVG from 0 to 100 percent draws upward. Made to Order interpolates three procedurally generated states (seed, layered stack, brilliant profile) that share identical point counts, instead of hand-authored MorphSVG states.
- Opening sequence (revised during review): the hero no longer pins. Its 100vh stage is held with position sticky inside a 230vh section (margin-bottom -100vh) so the time chapter rises over it after 30vh of scroll, which shortens the opening from 260vh to 130vh. The one WebGL stone became the opening stone (`src/components/sections/OpeningStone.tsx`, rendered inside the time chapter): a large brilliant in side profile, half off the hero's right edge at rest, gliding in and left as the chapter rises, settling in the chapter's left half beside the word weeks (the figure and copy now sit in the right half on desktop, and the digits paint in front of the stone), then leaving as the engagement chapter arrives. On phones the stone crosses to the centre and leaves with the hero. The wordmark is set left in the hero.
- Engagement (revised during review): the chapter no longer replays the hero photograph or opens through an aperture; it arrives dark and opens through a circle of light from the ring's place onto the porcelain stage with the copy, the scrubbed turntable (pin 150vh desktop, 110vh mobile), the shape strip and the settings row.
- The opening stone now uses the client-supplied glTF model at `public/media/models/diamond.glb` (a 126-triangle brilliant, normalised to girdle radius 1 and flat facet normals in `Diamond.tsx`; the procedural brilliant remains the fallback). It is a Sketchfab model under CC BY 4.0, see README credits.
- The hero diamond is a procedural round brilliant (`src/lib/diamondGeometry.ts`) rendered with drei's refraction material at two bounces and a faint aberration (the high tier) or a two-pass white crystal (the low tier), over a local Lightformer environment with an additive glint shell, pixel ratio capped at 1.25; it lazy-loads, caps device pixel ratio, pauses off screen, and on low-end devices the hero shows the photograph alone (no pre-rendered video fallback in this phase).
- The engagement turntable uses the 96-frame sequence at 900px (about 3MB) rather than 90 frames at 1200px; the settings tiles use the 720px videos with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` over paper.
- Shop-by-shape shows six shapes (Round, Oval, Emerald, Radiant, Princess, Pear) pending confirmation of the loose diamond search filters. Book a visit and Contact us open a contact drawer backed by a server action stub; no appointment system exists yet.
- No em dashes anywhere in copy or code comments. American spelling.

# Specification: Maison in Hard Light

A cool porcelain lookbook, opened and closed by the client's own hard-light studio photography: a dark first chapter where one glass diamond settles into a photographed ring and 1.5 billion years collapses into the word weeks, a light engagement aperture that turns the same ring under your hand, a horizontal collection, one dark flower chapter, and a single full-screen arrival of the brand's sky blue at the Fifth Avenue door.


## Palette
- `--porcelain` #F2F4F6: Page ground for every light section and the preloader. A cool off-white chosen to sit with the sky blue and the cold black-and-white photography; never warm ivory, never pure white.
- `--paper` #FAFBFC: Cards, product tiles, the Made to Order panel, mega menu surface, the contact drawer on light.
- `--hairline` #DCE1E6: Rules, dividers, card borders, the giant background line mark on light, inactive rails.
- `--stone` #8F9AA3: Captions, indices, disabled states, eyebrows on light, secondary text on light.
- `--graphite` #3A3F44: Body copy on light. Never pure black on porcelain.
- `--ink` #0B1F2A: Brand navy: headlines and wordmark on light, primary buttons on light, text on the sky panel, the dark stroke family of the line mark, footer ground.
- `--studio` #0A0B0C: Ground of the dark chapters (hero, time, the start of engagement, foreveryone, legacy). Kept a shade above pure black so the photographs' true blacks read deeper than the UI.
- `--carbon` #1C2228: Hairlines, card borders and rails on the dark chapters; the mobile menu tile border.
- `--sky` #86DFFF: Brand accent: the Visit and Trade panel ground, the word weeks, focus rings, cursor on dark, hover underline, the light stroke family of the line mark.
- `--line` #70C0E0: Second stroke colour of the line mark on light, link underlines, the education light ray, the mark's strokes on navy.
- `--mist` #DCEFF7: Pale sky tint for hover fills, selected shape tiles, the education row wash.
- `--beam` #E8F6FF: The preloader beam line, the diamond's specular and key light, the caustic disc on the hero floor, the traveling strand highlight.
- `--white` #FFFFFF: Render tiles, the turntable canvas ground and the white portrait cards on sky, because every product render and video is on pure white.

## Typography
- Display: Italiana 400, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em. Wordmark and single-word display moments only: hero wordmark clamp(88px, 15vw, 260px) at line-height 0.9; the time counter 1,500,000,000 at clamp(64px, 11vw, 176px) with font-variant-numeric tabular-nums; footer wordmark clamp(96px, 20vw, 360px); nav wordmark 20px with TM as a 7px superscript. One weight, no italic exists, so never fake one. Italiana never below 40px except the nav wordmark and never used for a sentence.
- Editorial: Cormorant Garamond 300 and 400 with 300 Italic. Section headlines clamp(40px, 5.6vw, 92px), line-height 1.02, letter-spacing -0.01em, sentence case with a full stop. Italic is reserved for one emphasised phrase inside a headline (one to three words, always the emotional word: forever, weeks, you, foreveryone), never a whole line and never body. Statement lines (lookbook) clamp(44px, 6.5vw, 112px) italic. Category names on cards 32px. Row titles 28 to 30px. Letter-spacing is never applied to Cormorant.
- UI: Jost 300 for body: 17px desktop, 16px mobile, line-height 1.6, max-width 34ch, graphite on light, porcelain at 78 percent on dark, ink on sky. Jost 400 for eyebrows (11px uppercase, tracking 0.22em, preceded by a 24px hairline that draws in), buttons and nav (12px uppercase, tracking 0.18em), captions (11px, tracking 0.08em), indices and counters (11px, tabular figures). Jost 500 only for the active nav item. Addresses and phone numbers in Jost 300 with tabular figures.
- Rules: Three faces, three jobs, never mixed inside one line: Italiana names and counts, Cormorant speaks, Jost operates. No weight above 500 anywhere. Maximum two type sizes per viewport besides captions. Headline measure 12 to 16 words. Body never wider than 34ch. Text on photography sits only on a region that is pure black or pure white, never on midtone; where none exists a 40 percent studio gradient scrim is added, never a box. Eyebrow, headline, body, CTA stack with 16 / 32 / 40px gaps. Every headline reveals by line (SplitText lines inside overflow-hidden masks, y 110 percent to 0, 1.1s, ease surreal, stagger 0.08s), never by character except the hero wordmark and the time counter. Body uses one 40px rise and fade over 1.2s. Font loading: next/font/google with size-adjust fallbacks, and every SplitText call waits for document.fonts.ready so lines are measured on the final face. No em dashes anywhere: commas, colons and full stops carry rhythm. American spelling throughout (color, jewelry).

## Preloader
Porcelain (#F2F4F6) full screen, so the mark appears exactly as the client drew it: 35 stroked SVG paths traced from Banners/Social Media 5.jpg, alternating ink #0B1F2A and sky #86DFFF, stroke-width 1.25 at 400px height, round caps, stable ids (s01 to s35, ordered bottom-left to top-right). Sequence, 2.2s total, driven by GSAP with one CustomEase named surreal (M0,0 C0.22,1 0.36,1 1,1). 0.0 to 1.5s: DrawSVGPlugin draws each stroke from its bottom-left origin to its diagonal tip, stagger 0.018s, each stroke 0.9s, ease power2.out, sky strokes leading their ink neighbours by 60ms so the mark seems lit from one side. 0.4 to 1.6s: a Jost 11px counter (000 to 100, tracking 0.3em, stone) runs bottom-left, driven by real asset progress via a promise on the hero photograph, the fonts, the diamond environment textures and the first 12 turntable frames, with a minimum display of 1.8s so it never flickers. 1.2 to 1.8s: SURREAL in Italiana 96px ink tracks in beneath the mark (letter-spacing 0.6em to 0.08em, opacity 0 to 1, power3.out), by JB BHANDERI in Jost 10px stone fading in 200ms later. 1.8 to 2.2s, the beam: a 2px beam-coloured vertical line with a 120px soft glow wipes left to right across the viewport; behind it the porcelain sheet is already gone (clip-path inset 0 0 0 X percent following the beam), so the dark hero appears in the beam's wake and the sweep reads as the light that turns the studio on. The mark and wordmark do not fade: with Flip they travel to their nav positions (mark to top-left at 28px tall, wordmark to 20px beside it), 0.9s, ease surreal, while the mark's ink strokes cross to porcelain over the last 0.4s because the nav has switched to data-theme dark over the hero (the sky strokes stay sky). Shown once per session (sessionStorage flag); return visits get a 0.4s porcelain fade and the nav mark already in place. prefers-reduced-motion: instant reveal, no draw, no counter, no beam, 300ms crossfade.

## Cursor and micro

Revised during review: the custom dot and ring cursor with labels was retired as too loud. The native cursor stays and a soft pool of beam-coloured light follows the pointer a beat behind it (screen blend, so it lifts the dark chapters and nearly vanishes on the light ones). The data-cursor attributes remain on elements for future use. The rest of this section still applies.
Custom cursor on pointer: fine devices only, hidden the moment a touch event fires: a 6px dot tracking 1:1 and a 32px 1px ring lagging with gsap.quickTo lerp 0.14. Ink on light, sky on dark, ink on the sky panel, swapped through the same data-theme. Over links and buttons the ring grows to 44px and the dot disappears (0.4s). Over cards, photographs and the horizontal track it grows to 72px with a Jost 10px uppercase label: View, Drag (collection track), Turn (engagement turntable), Play (setting tiles), Open (mega menu tiles). Over text it returns to the native I-beam; over inputs the ring becomes a filled mist disc. Magnetic buttons: within a 96px radius every pill and the nav icons drift toward the pointer up to 8px (quickTo x and y, 0.4s power3.out) and return with elastic.out(1, 0.6) on leave. Button styles, all Jost 12px uppercase tracking 0.18em, 52px tall, padding 0 32px: Primary on light is a filled ink pill whose porcelain fill rises from the bottom on hover (scaleY 0 to 1, transform-origin bottom, 0.5s) while the label slides up out of a mask and its duplicate arrives (y -100 percent, 0.45s) in ink; primary on dark is a filled porcelain pill inverting to ink the same way; primary on sky is a filled ink pill with sky text. Secondary is a 1px hairline outline pill in the current text colour that fills with mist on light (or a 12 percent porcelain fill on dark) on hover. Tertiary is a text link with the drawn line underline and a small arrow that slides 6px right. There is no liquid metal, chrome or metallic button anywhere: the only metal on the page is the jewelry. Cards lift 6px with a shadow from 0 to 24px blur at 8 percent ink, their image scales 1.04 over 1.0s, their arrow translates 6px. Image reveal, shared everywhere: clip-path inset(0 0 100 percent 0) to inset(0) over 1.3s surreal while the image scales 1.12 to 1, triggered at 80 percent viewport. Inputs: hairline underline turns sky on focus (0.3s) and the label floats up. Scroll indicator: a 48px vertical hairline whose inner beam segment loops top to bottom every 2.4s. Lenis lerp 0.08, wheelMultiplier 1, smoothTouch off, ticker bound to gsap.ticker with lagSmoothing 0; anchors and Back to top use lenis.scrollTo with ease surreal. Every ScrollTrigger scrub is between 0.4 and 1.2, every reveal 1.1 to 1.4s, every exit 0.6s. Only transform, opacity and clip-path animate; will-change is applied at pin start and removed at pin end. prefers-reduced-motion: Lenis off, cursor off, pins become static stacks, scrubs show their final frame, reveals become 0.3s opacity fades, the hero diamond renders one static frame, all hover motion off.

## Section order rationale
The page is built as a deliberate rhythm of light: dark, dark, opening, light, light, light, dark, dark, light, light, sky, sky, navy. It opens in the studio because that is the register of the client's photography and the client asked for an immersive first impression: the hero (the diamond settling into the ring) and the time counter (the one fact only a grower can say) are the two dark beats a visitor must pass before any decision. Engagement is third because it is the highest-value door on the site and because its aperture is the page's emotional turn: the same ring the visitor just saw opens the room from dark into light, so the transition itself sells the product. The page then stays light for the three commerce and program sections that need a light ground to convert: the collection gallery (all nine categories, fourth, ahead of every program), Made to Order (the grower's unique offer, told in the mark's strokes on paper), and the lookbook (the everyday luxury shown on real people, with a product link under every image). The second dark chapter arrives only after six monochrome sections, so the flower photographs land as the first and only colour: foreveryone carries the inclusive promise and the fine jewelry door, and Legacy shares its ground because the family program is the most emotional beat and belongs in the dark with the green bud. Light returns for the two service sections that need reading room, Custom (craft, sketch to setting) and Education (the guide and FAQs), placed after the products they explain. The brand's sky blue is held back until the two physical and trade doors at the end: Visit opens it as a circle from the bottom, rhyming with the engagement aperture, and Trade continues on it with the mark drawing the panel in, so the story that began in carbon ends in sky. The footer closes on the brand navy with the mark redrawing exactly as it did in the preloader, and Back to top remounts the hero diamond, so the film loops.

## Risks
- The hero MeshTransmissionMaterial diamond is the single most expensive element and the only WebGL context on the page; it must lazy-load, cap DPR at 1.5, run frameloop demand, unmount 100vh after the hero pin, and hand over to the pre-rendered transparent WebM on integrated GPUs and under 4GB device memory, otherwise the smoothness the client asked for is lost on the laptops buyers use.
- Four pins (hero 160vh, time 150vh, engagement 240vh, collection about 330vh) total roughly 880vh, with three of them consecutive at the top of the page; test on a trackpad and a mouse wheel separately with Lenis, and if the opening feels held, fold the time counter into the hero pin so there is one boundary instead of two.
- The Made to Order MorphSVG across 35 strokes and three states is the hardest vector job in the spec; author all three states from one master with identical anchor counts and direction, keep the DrawSVG crossfade fallback per path pair, and budget a full day for the mark trace plus the three morph states.
- The engagement frame sequence (90 webp frames, about 3.5MB) must be fetched during the time section with a 12-frame eager prefetch and fall back to video currentTime scrubbing if fewer than 60 frames have arrived by pin start; also confirm the video's white ground multiplies cleanly onto porcelain after a levels pass, otherwise keep the canvas on pure white.
- The dark chapters rising over held light sections use CSS position sticky holds, not ScrollTrigger pins, to avoid nested pinning with Lenis; still test iOS Safari address bar resize and z-index stacking on the lookbook to foreveryone and legacy to custom seams.
- The shop-by-shape strip (Round, Oval, Emerald, Radiant, Princess, Pear) is inferred from the render and video library, not a stated client list; confirm the shapes the loose diamond search supports and the search URL pattern before build so no tile leads to an empty result.
- Text on photography (3984 in the hero, 4117 in legacy, 3605 and 3730 interludes) must land on true black or true white at every breakpoint; check the crops at 1280, 1440, 1920 and mobile before code, and export the 3984 21:9 and 4:5 crops plus webp sizes at 2400, 1600 and 960 for every photograph first.
- Copy uses only verified claims (grower, CVD, DEF and VVS, weeks, identical to mined, the programs, the New York office, hours and phone); Book a visit and Contact us route to a contact form, not an appointment system, and any new line added in client review must be checked against the brief's unknowns: no founding year, facility location, certifications, prices or carat ranges.
# Specification: The Collection (shop pages)

Routes: `/jewelry` (every piece) and `/jewelry/[category]` for engagement, wedding-bands, earrings, necklaces, pendants, rings, bracelets and sterling-silver (the ids in `src/content/catalog.ts`). Both render the same client page with the category preselected. Light chapter throughout (porcelain ground, ink type), same chrome as the homepage (header with `initialTheme="light"`, cursor, contact drawer), no preloader. No prices, no cart: the doors are "Enquire about this piece" (contact drawer) and "Book a visit". Copy is factual and short; the same three voices and rules as the homepage; no em dashes.

## 01. Masthead (`shop-masthead`)
- Layout: 64px gutters, min 62vh. Top: eyebrow "The collection" (on a category page the eyebrow is "The collection" and the headline is the category). Headline in Cormorant 300 clamp(56px, 7.5vw, 128px): "Nine ways to <em>begin</em>." for all pieces, else the category label with its last word in italic (for example "Engagement <em>rings</em>.", "Wedding <em>bands</em>.", "Sterling <em>silver</em>."). Under it, columns 1 to 5: the category blurb from `categories` (or, for all: "Earrings, engagement rings, wedding bands, necklaces, pendants, rings, bracelets and a sterling silver collection. Every piece set with a diamond we grew.") at 34ch, and a Jost 11 tabular count line "84 pieces" that updates with the filters.
- Category rail: below the headline, a horizontal row of nine tiles (All plus the eight categories): each a 112px paper circle holding the category's render on multiply (All uses a hairline plan-view brilliant), label beneath in Jost 11 uppercase; the active tile carries a 1px ink ring and a sky dot; hover lifts 4px and scales the render 1.06. Tiles are links to the routes. On mobile the rail scrolls horizontally with scroll-snap.
- Texture: the LineMark at 70vh tall, ink at 6 percent, bleeding off the top right behind the headline (decorative, aria-hidden), drawing in with DrawSVG on load.
- Motion: headline lines mask-reveal, blurb and count fade up, tiles stagger 0.05 from the left with a 24px rise.

## 02. Toolbar (`shop-toolbar`)
- Sticky under the header (top: var(--nav-h)), porcelain at 88 percent with backdrop blur, 1px hairline below, 64px height desktop.
- Left: filter groups as chip rows in Jost 12 uppercase 0.18em: Metal (White gold, Yellow gold, Rose gold), Shape (Round, Oval, Emerald, Radiant, Princess, Pear; shown only when the category is all or engagement). A chip is a hairline pill; active fills ink with porcelain text (on sky pages ink). Each group is single select: a chip behaves like a radio (choosing one clears the group's other chip, choosing the active chip clears the group), groups are AND. A "Clear" text link appears when anything is active.
- Right: "Sort" as a minimal native select styled as a text link with a chevron (Featured, A to Z, Z to A); a density toggle of three small glyphs (2, 3, 4 columns) desktop only, default 3; the count in Jost 11 tabular.
- Filters and sort live in the query string (`?metal=yg&shape=oval&sort=az`, one value per group; an older comma list is read as its first value) through `useSearchParams` and `router.replace` with scroll false, so a filtered view can be shared. Below 768px the chip rows collapse into a "Filter" pill that opens a bottom sheet (same drawer mechanics as the contact drawer: paper, slides up, Escape closes, focus trapped) with the groups stacked and an "Apply" primary pill.

## 03. Grid (`shop-grid`)
- CSS grid, gap 24px, columns from the density (2, 3 or 4; 2 below 768px with 12px gap, 1 below 420px). Cards keep a 4:5 paper tile with a 1px hairline, an index top-left (Jost 11 tabular, 01, 02, ...), and a small "360" mark top-right when the piece has a turntable video. The render sits on multiply at 74 percent of the tile width. Beneath the tile: name in Cormorant 400 22px, detail in Jost 12 stone, then the card's selectors (see Selectors and placeholders below).
- Hover (fine pointers): the tile lifts 6px with a soft shadow, the render scales 1.05; if the piece has `angles`, the render crossfades to the first angle; if it has `video`, the turntable plays muted on loop (720px mp4 on multiply) and stops on leave; both only while the card's selection has a render. The tile and caption open Quick View on click (`data-cursor="view"`); the selectors beneath do not.
- Interludes inside the grid, in a fixed rhythm: after every eighth card a 2-column editorial tile (aspect 8:5, hairline, next/image cover, parallax 0.12) with a caption in Cormorant italic 28px and a tertiary link, cycling through: sb-3605 "Worn every day" (Shop earrings), sb-3730 "Gold, stacked" (Shop rings), sb-3614 "Made to be worn" (Shop necklaces), sb-4074 "Light, held" (Shop earrings), sb-3631 "On every hand" (Shop rings). After the sixteenth card, once, a dark promo tile (studio ground, 2 columns, aspect 8:5): the LineMark small in sky and porcelain at the left, eyebrow "Made to Order", headline in Cormorant 300 32px "Do not see the stone you want? We grow it.", body "Tell us the shape, the size and the color and we grow the stone to your specification.", primary porcelain pill "Start a made to order diamond" to /made-to-order.
- Reveal: cards clip from the bottom (inset 100% 0 0 0 to 0) with a 24px rise, stagger 0.06 in ScrollTrigger batches. Filtering, sorting and density changes reflow with GSAP Flip (record state, update, `Flip.from` with duration 0.7, ease surreal, absolute, entering cards fade and rise in, leaving cards fade and scale to 0.96).
- Show 24 pieces first, then a secondary pill "Show more" (24 at a time) with the count "24 of 84"; the URL is not changed by paging. Empty state (no matches): a centred Cormorant line "Nothing matches these filters yet." with a "Clear filters" tertiary link.

## 04. Quick view (`shop-quick-view`)

A "Previous" and "Next" pair in the drawer head steps through the current filtered list.

## Selectors and placeholders
- Filters are single select: one metal and one shape at most, so the query string is `?metal=yg&shape=oval`; whenever a metal is filtered every card starts on that metal.
- Every card carries its own selectors beneath the name and detail: a metal row of the three metals as 18px swatch dots (metal name as the accessible label, the active one ringed in ink) and a carat row of small hairline chips from `caratOptions[category]` formatted with `formatCarat` (active filled ink). Defaults are the first of `productMetals` (or the filtered metal) and the first carat; the selection is local to the card, keyed by product id, and travels into the quick view when the card opens it.
- The stage shows a render only for the piece's own metals at the first carat (the variant, else the primary image), crossfading over 0.35s. Any other combination shows the placeholder on the same paper tile: a small hairline plan-view brilliant in ink at 40 percent, then "Made to order in rose gold, 2 ct." in Cormorant (22px on the card, 28px on the quick view stage) and "No render yet. Enquire and we will send one." in Jost 12 stone. Hover angle and turntable behaviors apply only while the selection has a render.
- The carat ranges in `caratOptions` are placeholders pending the client's confirmation.
